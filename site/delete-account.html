Delete my account and data

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This page explains how to delete your PlantMaster Pro account, as required by Google Play's data deletion policy.

Option 1 — from inside the app

Workspace owners can request deletion of the whole company workspace from Plan & Usage → Request data deletion. Individual members should ask their workspace owner to deactivate them, which immediately removes their access.

Option 2 — by email

Send a request from your registered work email to support@hsbfix.org with the subject "Account deletion request", stating your company name. We verify the request with the workspace owner before acting.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, phone, designation, employee ID and skills.
  • Your membership of the workspace and all access.
  • If a whole workspace is deleted: assets, work orders, checklists, attendance, spares, purchase orders, uploaded manuals, scans and condition recordings.

What is retained, and for how long

  • Audit records of safety-relevant actions (for example who approved a permit to work) are retained in anonymised form for up to 7 years, because industrial safety and statutory record-keeping require it.
  • Billing records are retained for the period required by Pakistani tax law.

Timeline

Deletion requests are actioned within 30 days. Records first move to a recovery bin so an accidental request can be reversed, then are permanently removed. You will receive written confirmation when deletion is complete.
