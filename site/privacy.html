Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This policy explains what PlantMaster Pro collects, why, and what control you have. It also covers the disclosures Google Play requires.

1. What we collect

  • Account data — name, work email, phone, designation, employee ID, shift and role within your company workspace.
  • Operational data you enter — assets, work orders, checklists, attendance, spares, purchase orders, daily logs and similar maintenance records.
  • Files you upload — equipment manuals, drawings, photos, scanned documents and audio recordings you make for condition monitoring.
  • Technical data — device type, browser/app version and error diagnostics, used to keep the app working.
  • Audit records — who changed which record and when. This is a core feature and cannot be switched off within a workspace.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect precise location. We do not use advertising identifiers, run third-party ad networks, or sell or rent your data to anyone.

3. Camera, microphone and notifications

  • Camera — used only when you scan a QR code or photograph a document or fault. Images go to your workspace storage; the camera is never used in the background.
  • Microphone — used only when you record machine sound for condition monitoring, or use voice input to dictate a field. Recording is explicit and visible.
  • Notifications — used to deliver work-order assignments and plant alarms. You can decline or revoke this at any time in your device settings.

4. How your data is protected

Data is stored with Supabase (PostgreSQL) and transmitted over TLS. Every table is protected by row-level security so a workspace can only ever read its own rows; this is enforced by the database itself, not only by the app. Files are stored in a private bucket and served through short-lived signed links. Access inside your company is controlled by the role your owner assigns you.

5. AI processing

When you use AI troubleshooting or condition analysis, the relevant question and context — which may include extracts from manuals you uploaded and the plant history of the machine concerned — are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. This is processing on our behalf for your request. Your data is not used to train third-party models. If you prefer not to use AI features, simply do not use them; the rest of the app works without them.

6. Who else processes data

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage.
  • Cloudflare — hosting and content delivery for the web app.
  • Google (Gemini API) — AI responses, only when you use an AI feature.
  • Resend — delivery of the optional daily email report.

7. Retention

Operational records are retained for as long as your workspace is active, and by default for the periods set in your workspace retention policy (operational records 3 years, audit records 7 years, AI prompts 1 year). Deleted records go to a recovery bin for a grace period (30 days by default) before permanent deletion.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Workspace owners can export company data at any time from the app. To delete your account see Delete my account. Contact privacy@hsbfix.org for any privacy request.

9. Children

The Service is for workplace use and is not directed at anyone under 18.

10. Changes

We will notify you in the app of material changes to this policy.
