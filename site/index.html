Everything a maintenance department actually needs

🧰 Work orders

Raise, assign, and close corrective work with downtime, labour and cost captured. Permits to work and lock-out/tag-out are enforced by the system, not by memory.

🏭 Asset register

Every machine with its code, rating, manuals and full repair history. Print a QR sticker and any technician can pull it up in two seconds.

✅ Checklists & rounds

Shift rounds with limits on every reading. An out-of-range value raises an alarm to supervisors immediately.

🛠 Preventive maintenance

Weekly, monthly and running-hour based plans that generate work automatically and track what was really done.

📦 Spares & procurement

Stock with minimum levels, tool custody, material requests, purchase orders and goods receipt — stock updates itself as goods arrive.

🎧 Condition monitoring

Record machine sound and vibration with the phone you already carry. Compare against a healthy baseline and get an early warning before failure.

🤖 AI troubleshooting

Ask in plain language or Urdu voice. Answers are grounded in your uploaded manuals and your plant's own solved cases — with safety steps first.

📊 Reports & audit

Daily email summaries, MTTR and downtime analytics, and an immutable audit trail you can export to CSV, Excel, Word or PDF.
