Compressor tripping on high discharge temperature after 20 minutes. Oil level is normal. What should I check?
Check the aftercooler and oil cooler for fouling first — high discharge temperature with normal oil level usually points to restricted heat exchange rather than lubrication. Verify cooling water flow is at least 45 L/min and confirm the thermostatic valve is opening. If both are correct, inspect the minimum pressure valve for sticking. Source: Atlas Copco GA-75 Service Manual, section 4.3 — Fault Finding

The same question, asked in two accounts

One account had no equipment manuals loaded. The other had the plant's own compressor documentation indexed. Below is what each one actually returned.

Question typed by the technician: “Compressor isn't providing the 8 bar pressure even the capacity of compressors is 10 bar”

Account with no manuals loaded

General engineering knowledge only
Confidence 0.85 0 plant sources
Diagnostic answer
“…typically indicates a leak, mechanical wear, or control system limitation. Check for air leaks in the discharge piping, valves and fittings. Inspect the air intake filter for clogs. Verify the pressure switch or transducer calibration. Check for worn piston rings or leaking valves (reciprocating) or air-end wear (rotary screw).”
Likely causes
  • Air leaks in the discharge line or downstream system
  • Clogged air intake filter
  • Faulty pressure switch or transducer calibration
  • Worn piston rings or leaking valves (reciprocating)
  • Clogged oil separator or minimum pressure valve (rotary screw)
  • Slipping drive belts
Sources PlantMaster General Compressor Maintenance Guidelines · Standard Industrial Pneumatic Troubleshooting Protocol

Account with its own manuals loaded

Grounded in the plant's own documentation
Confidence 0.90 8 plant sources
Diagnostic answer
“…is likely due to incorrect controller setpoints, a significant downstream air leak, or a restricted intake filter. First, verify the ‘Unloading Pressure’ parameter in the controller menu (MAM 6080) to ensure it is not set to 8 bar. Second, inspect the system for air leaks and check the intake air filter for blockages.”
Safe checks, in order
  • Verify ‘Unloading Pressure’ parameter in the MAM 6080 controller menu
  • Perform a leak test on the downstream air distribution system
  • Inspect and clean/replace the air intake filter
  • Check pressure transducer readings against a calibrated gauge
  • Inspect the unloading valve for proper seating
Sources Jaguar 55KW instruction manual, pages 28–31 · Oil-free compressor 75 K OF part manual · Maintenance Schedule — GA compressors and FX Dryer
Both answers are competent. Only one of them tells the technician which menu to open on the controller in front of him — and shows the page it came from, so he can check it himself. The generic answer sends him hunting through five possibilities; the grounded one starts with a setting that is often the whole problem, and no parts are replaced unnecessarily.

Questions

What plant managers ask us

How does the AI actually know about our machines?

You upload the PDF manuals for your equipment. The system reads and indexes every page. When a technician asks a question, the answer is built from those documents and tells you which manual and section it came from. It is not guessing from the internet — it is quoting your own paperwork.

Do my technicians need to be good with computers?

No. If they can use WhatsApp they can use PlantMaster. Large buttons, few screens, and voice input for anyone who does not want to type. Most teams are working confidently after a twenty-minute walkthrough.

Do we need to buy computers, tablets or sensors?

No. It runs in the browser on any Android phone your staff already carry. Condition monitoring records machine sound through the phone's microphone and compares it against a baseline you set — it is an early-warning aid, not a replacement for calibrated vibration analysis.

What happens when the internet or power goes?

If the connection drops briefly while you are saving a record, the entry is held on the phone and sent automatically once the signal returns, so the work is not lost. A working internet connection is needed for normal day-to-day use.

How long does setup take?

An afternoon for most plants. We create your company, add your plants and import your asset list and spares from Excel for you at no charge.

Is our data safe, and who can see it?

Your data is isolated from every other company at the database level, not just hidden in the interface. Inside your company, access is controlled by role — owner, manager, supervisor, technician. You can export everything at any time.

Can it connect to our accounts or ERP software?

Yes. PlantMaster has a REST API so your accounts system can read assets, work orders, spares, suppliers and purchase orders automatically. Your IT person or software vendor gets an API key and documentation.

What if we want to stop?

Cancel whenever you like. No lock-in contract, no cancellation fee. Export your complete data to Excel first — it is yours and we will help you get it out.
