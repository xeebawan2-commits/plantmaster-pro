================================================================
 DO YOU NEED DOCUMENTS? — SHORT ANSWER: NO
 What Capterra and G2 actually check before approving
================================================================

NO PAPERWORK IS REQUIRED.

  No business registration certificate
  No NTN / tax number
  No incorporation papers
  No bank statement
  No ID card or passport
  No customer contracts
  No invoices

These are free software directory listings, not a payment
processor or a bank. Nobody is doing KYC on you.

Do not send documents even if a salesperson later suggests it.


----------------------------------------------------------------
 WHAT THEY ACTUALLY VERIFY: YOUR WEBSITE
----------------------------------------------------------------

Their catalog team opens hsbfix.org and decides from public
information alone. Gartner states plainly that they evaluate
"using publicly available information, such as a vendor's
website" and that they do NOT accept internal documents.

So your website IS the application. I audited it against every
published rejection reason, live, just now:

  1. Public website, in English                  PASS
  2. Product clearly described on the site       PASS
  3. Listing name matches site name              PASS (12x)
  4. Transparent pricing published               PASS
  5. Real CTA - trial AND demo, not a waitlist   PASS
  6. Company-domain email, not Gmail             PASS
  7. Sold as a suite, not separate modules       PASS
  8. B2B prepackaged, not B2C or bespoke         PASS

All eight clear. This is why the demo page and the pricing
page mattered so much - they are the two things that most
often get a new vendor rejected.


----------------------------------------------------------------
 WHAT THEY WILL ASK YOU, IN THE FORM
----------------------------------------------------------------

Only ordinary business details. All of it is already written
out for you in SUBMIT-TODAY.txt:

  Company name, your name, role, phone number
  Company-domain email      -> support@hsbfix.org
  Website, demo URL, pricing URL
  Product name and category
  200-300 word description  (written, compliance-checked)
  Pricing tiers             (written)
  Feature checklist         (written)
  Logo + screenshots        (ready in LISTING-ASSETS)

Nothing there needs a lawyer or an accountant.


----------------------------------------------------------------
 WHAT THEY WILL ASK AFTERWARDS: A SALES CALL
----------------------------------------------------------------

Expect a call or email within roughly a week of approval.
This is the only real "inquiry" you will get.

They will ask:
  - how many customers you have
  - your monthly marketing budget
  - whether you want pay-per-click placement

Answer honestly. Zero customers is fine - plenty of listed
vendors are early stage. It does not affect your free listing.

Then decline the paid tier. Pay-per-click starts at USD 2 per
click with a USD 500/month minimum, roughly Rs 138,000 a
month. That is indefensible with no reviews on the profile.

Say: "We will stay on the free Launch listing for now."

They may push twice. The free listing is not withdrawn if you
say no - it is a real product tier, not bait.


----------------------------------------------------------------
 THE ONE HONEST RISK
----------------------------------------------------------------

Gartner PEER INSIGHTS (a different Gartner product) requires
vendors to sell to mid-to-large enterprises and excludes small
business solutions. If anyone steers you there, you will not
qualify yet.

That is NOT what you are applying to. Capterra, GetApp and
Software Advice explicitly serve small business and mid-market
buyers, and that is where SUBMIT-TODAY.txt points you.

Use ONLY this URL:  https://www.capterra.com/vendors/sign-up


----------------------------------------------------------------
 IF THEY DO COME BACK WITH A QUESTION
----------------------------------------------------------------

The likeliest queries, and your answers:

  "Is this generally available or still in beta?"
     Generally available. Live product, paying tiers
     published, working demo, 7-day trial.

  "Who are your customers?"
     Industrial plants in Pakistan - textile mills, food
     processing, manufacturing. Early stage. Say so plainly.

  "Can we see the product?"
     https://hsbfix.org/demo.html - no login needed.
     Or app.hsbfix.org/?demo=1 with the demo credentials.
     This is your strongest answer. Most early vendors
     cannot show a working product at all.

  "Do you integrate with anything?"
     Not yet. Say nothing rather than invent integrations -
     a false claim can pull the listing later.

Answer in plain English, keep it short, do not oversell.


----------------------------------------------------------------
 BOTTOM LINE
----------------------------------------------------------------

Nothing is blocking you. No documents to gather, nothing to
prepare, no registration to obtain first.

The work is done. What remains is about 30 minutes of typing
into their form, copying from SUBMIT-TODAY.txt.
