REBRAND UPLOAD — new logo + blue/amber palette
==============================================

Your hexagon logo is now the icon everywhere, and the whole
product has been recoloured from teal/cyan to the blues in it.

  website  teal   #0F9B8E  ->  blue  #2563EB
  app      cyan   #22D3EE  ->  blue  #3B82F6
  admin    teal   #13B8A8  ->  blue  #3B82F6
  accent                       amber #D97706  (from the wrench)
  success                      green #10B981  (from the dot)

43 colour swaps. Zero teal or cyan left anywhere.

Colours were chosen with contrast maths, not by eye:
  #2563EB on white    5.2:1  passes, white button text passes
  #3B82F6 on #0D1117  5.1:1  passes
  #F59E0B on white    2.1:1  FAILS - so amber is never used for
                             text or buttons on the light site.
                             It survives as the PRO wordmark, a
                             divider rule, and the dark-mode
                             warning colour only.
