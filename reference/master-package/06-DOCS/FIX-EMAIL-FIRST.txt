I checked your DNS. There is a problem that would have wasted
days, and it is better you know now than after submitting.

  hsbfix.org        MX records:  NONE
  send.hsbfix.org   MX records:  send.forge.rmta.net  (Resend)

Resend is a SENDING service. It pushes your daily report
emails out. It does not give you an inbox.

  => support@hsbfix.org can SEND but CANNOT RECEIVE.

With no MX record on the root domain, any mail sent to
support@hsbfix.org is rejected by the sending server before it
is even delivered. It does not land in spam. It does not land
anywhere. It bounces.

G2 and Capterra both work by emailing you a verification link.
You would sit waiting for a link that physically cannot arrive.


----------------------------------------------------------------
 THE FIX — FREE, ABOUT 15 MINUTES
----------------------------------------------------------------

Your domain is at Porkbun, and Porkbun gives 20 free email
forwarding addresses with every domain. Forwarding means mail
sent to support@hsbfix.org is delivered into your existing
Gmail inbox.

  1. Log in to porkbun.com
  2. Domain Management -> hsbfix.org
  3. Find "Email Forwarding" -> Add
  4. Forward:  support@hsbfix.org
     To:       your working Gmail address
  5. Save.

Porkbun adds the MX records for you automatically.

  6. Wait 15-30 minutes for DNS to propagate.
  7. TEST IT. From your Gmail, send a message to
     support@hsbfix.org. If it appears back in your inbox,
     it works. Do not skip this test.

While you are there, add these too - all free, all useful:
     info@hsbfix.org      -> same Gmail
     sales@hsbfix.org     -> same Gmail
     xeebawan2@hsbfix.org -> same Gmail

WILL THIS BREAK RESEND?
  No. Resend sends from send.hsbfix.org, a subdomain with its
  own MX record. Adding MX records to the root domain does not
  touch it. Your daily report emails keep working exactly as
  they do now.

  One caution: do NOT delete or edit anything under
  send.hsbfix.org while you are in the DNS panel.
