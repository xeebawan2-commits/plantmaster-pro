================================================================
 YOUR EMAIL FORWARDS — ONE CHANGE NEEDED
================================================================

WHAT YOU HAVE NOW
-----------------
  hello@hsbfix.org    -> hsbfix.pk@gmail.com
  info@hsbfix.org     -> hsbfix.pk@gmail.com
  sales@hsbfix.org    -> hsbfix.pk@gmail.com
  support@hsbfix.org  -> xeebawan2@gmail.com    <-- odd one out

All four work. MX records are healthy. Nothing is broken.


THE ONE PROBLEM
---------------
support@hsbfix.org is printed on ALL SEVEN pages of your
website. It is the address customers will actually use. It is
also the address you will give Capterra and G2.

Right now it is the ONLY one landing in a different inbox from
the other three. Your customer mail is split across two Gmail
accounts, and the most important address is the one that is
separated.

That is exactly how a first enquiry gets missed.


THE FIX — 60 SECONDS
--------------------
Point support@ at the business Gmail, same as the rest.

  1. In Porkbun, click the trash icon next to
     support@hsbfix.org -> xeebawan2@gmail.com
  2. In "Add Forwarding Address":
       When someone sends an email to:  support
       Deliver it to:                   hsbfix.pk@gmail.com
  3. Create Email Forward

Result — one inbox, everything in it:

  hello@    -> hsbfix.pk@gmail.com
  info@     -> hsbfix.pk@gmail.com
  sales@    -> hsbfix.pk@gmail.com
  support@  -> hsbfix.pk@gmail.com


WHY THE BUSINESS GMAIL, NOT THE PERSONAL ONE
--------------------------------------------
  - hsbfix.pk@gmail.com is the business account. If you ever
    hand support to someone else, you hand over one login.
  - xeebawan2@gmail.com is your personal account and is also
    your Supabase super-admin login. Keep customer traffic
    away from it.
  - One inbox means one place to check. Four addresses feeding
    two inboxes is how things get missed.

KEEP xeebawan2@gmail.com FOR:
  Supabase, GitHub, Porkbun, billing. Your admin identity.
  Not customer mail.


DO NOT ADD demo@hsbfix.org
--------------------------
demo@hsbfix.org appears on demo.html, but it is a LOGIN for
the read-only demo account, not a mailbox. It never needs to
receive anything. Adding a forward for it would be harmless
but pointless.


AFTER YOU MAKE THE CHANGE
-------------------------
Set ONE Gmail filter in hsbfix.pk@gmail.com that covers all
four addresses at once:

  Gmail -> Settings -> Filters and Blocked Addresses
  -> Create a new filter
     To:  hsbfix.org
          (just the domain — this matches every address on it)
  -> Create filter
  -> tick "Never send it to Spam"
  -> tick "Always mark it as important"
  -> Create filter

That single filter covers hello@, info@, sales@, support@ and
anything you add later. It also solves the spam problem you
saw earlier, permanently.

Then send one test to support@hsbfix.org from an address that
is NOT hsbfix.pk@gmail.com, and confirm it lands in the inbox
rather than spam.


WHAT TO GIVE THE DIRECTORIES
----------------------------
  Capterra / Gartner form:  support@hsbfix.org
  G2:                       support@hsbfix.org

Both are on your own domain, which is what they require. Do
not give them a raw Gmail address.


OPTIONAL, NOT NOW
-----------------
Forwarding lets you RECEIVE as support@hsbfix.org, but replies
will show your Gmail address as the sender. That is fine for
verification links and early customer mail.

If you later want replies to come FROM support@hsbfix.org, you
have two routes:
  - Zoho Mail free tier (1 user, real mailbox), or
  - Gmail "Send mail as" using Resend's SMTP
      host smtp.resend.com, port 465,
      username: the literal word  resend
      password: your Resend API key
Both are jobs for after the listings are filed.
