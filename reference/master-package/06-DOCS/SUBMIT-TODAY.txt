 DESCRIPTION — paste exactly, do not reword
----------------------------------------------------------------

Checked against all seven rejection rules: 203 words, no
first person, no superlatives, no call to action, no URL,
no phone, no company suffix, no symbols.

PlantMaster Pro is maintenance management software for
industrial plants, built for textile mills, food processing
units, and manufacturing sites in South Asia. It covers work
orders, preventive maintenance schedules, asset registers with
QR code scanning, spare parts inventory, purchase orders, and
technician attendance.

The system includes an AI troubleshooting assistant that reads
the equipment manuals a plant uploads and answers fault
questions from those documents, citing the page number the
answer came from. Where a manual does not cover the question,
the assistant reports that rather than generating an
untraceable answer.

A condition monitoring module uses a standard smartphone
microphone to record a thirty second sample from running
equipment and compares it against a baseline recorded when the
machine was healthy, highlighting the frequency bands that have
changed. No additional sensors or wiring are required.

Voice input accepts both Urdu and English, allowing shop floor
staff to raise jobs by speaking rather than typing. A daily
summary email reports open work, overdue tasks, and equipment
flagged for attention.

Pricing is charged per plant rather than per user, with four
tiers covering small workshops through to multi site
operations. The software runs in a web browser on any phone,
tablet or desktop without installation.
