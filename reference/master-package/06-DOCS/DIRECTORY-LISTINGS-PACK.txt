    PlantMaster Pro is maintenance management software for
    industrial plants, built for textile mills, food

    It covers work orders, preventive maintenance schedules,
    asset registers with QR code scanning, spare parts
    inventory, purchase orders, and technician attendance.

    The system includes an AI troubleshooting assistant that
    reads the equipment manuals a plant uploads and answers
    fault questions from those documents, citing the page
    number the answer came from. Where a manual does not
    cover the question, the assistant reports that rather
    than generating an untraceable answer.

    A condition monitoring module uses a standard smartphone
    microphone to record a thirty second sample from running
    equipment and compares it against a baseline recorded
    when the machine was healthy, highlighting the frequency
    bands that have changed. No additional sensors or wiring
    are required.

    Voice input accepts both Urdu and English, allowing shop
    floor staff to raise jobs by speaking rather than typing.
    A daily summary email reports open work, overdue tasks,
    and equipment flagged for attention.

    Pricing is charged per plant rather than per user, with
    four tiers covering small workshops through to multi
    site operations. The software runs in a web browser on
    any phone, tablet or desktop without installation.

----------------------------------------------------------------
 4. THINGS THAT WILL GET YOU BANNED - read this
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Do not write your own review, or have staff or family write
  one. Both platforms detect this through IP address, device
  fingerprint and writing style. The penalty is removal of
  the listing, and it is permanent.

  Do not offer payment for a positive review. Offering a
  small thank-you gift for an HONEST review is allowed on
  Capterra, but it must be offered to everyone regardless of
  what they write, and it must be disclosed.

  Do not copy competitor descriptions.

  Do not use "best", "fastest", "leading", "number one".
  Listings get edited or rejected for superlatives.

  Do not put a phone number, email or URL inside the
  description text. There are separate fields for those.

  Do not claim integrations you do not have.
