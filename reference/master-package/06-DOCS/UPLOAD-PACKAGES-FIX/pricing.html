Pricing questions

Before you ask

Which plan should we start on?

If you are coming off paper registers, Essential proves the system works with your team. If you already keep decent records and your problem is unplanned breakdowns, go straight to Professional — the condition monitoring and AI are the parts that change outcomes rather than just documenting them.

Can we upgrade later?

Yes, any time, and it takes effect immediately with the difference prorated. Nothing is re-entered and no data moves — the extra features simply switch on. Downgrading applies at your next renewal.

Does the trial include the AI?

Yes. All 7 days run on full Professional features so you can judge the AI and condition monitoring properly. You choose a plan at the end, not the beginning.

What counts as a user?

Anyone who signs in — technicians, supervisors, managers, owners. Suppliers and outside contractors do not need accounts, so they cost nothing.

We have 18 staff. What do we pay?

Professional covers you to 25 users at Rs 24,000. If you only want Essential, extra users are added in blocks of five. Send us a message and we will give you a straight number for your actual headcount.

Does the AI cost extra per question?

No. On Professional, asking the AI and running condition analysis are included with no credits to buy and no per-question charge. There is fair-use monitoring to stop genuine abuse, nothing more.

What happens after the 7 days?

We contact you and ask how it went. If you want to continue we issue an invoice. If not, nothing happens — no card was taken and no billing starts. You can export your data either way.

Is there a discount for paying yearly?

Yes — two months free, so you pay for ten and get twelve. Switch the toggle above to see the yearly figures.

What about a small workshop with three people?

Send us a WhatsApp message. If you are genuinely small we will find a price that works, because we would rather have you using it than not.
