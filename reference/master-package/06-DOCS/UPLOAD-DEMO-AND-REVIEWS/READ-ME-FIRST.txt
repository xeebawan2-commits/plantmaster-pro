WHAT THE DEMO IS

  https://hsbfix.org/demo.html

  A working click-through of the app, loaded with one week of
  sample data from a fictional Karachi spinning mill. Six
  tabs: Dashboard, Work Orders, Assets, AI Problem Solver,
  Condition Monitoring, Manuals.

  Everything runs inside the page. It never touches your
  database, never touches Supabase, and cannot be broken by a
  visitor. There is nothing to log into.

  Things a visitor can actually do:
    - click through all six screens
    - ask the AI solver three sample questions, including one
      in Urdu, and watch it answer with a manual page number
    - press "Record 30 seconds" on condition monitoring and
      watch the frequency comparison appear

  An orange strip across the top says it is demo data from a
  fictional mill, so nobody can accuse you of inventing
  customers.


WHY IT MATTERS MORE THAN IT LOOKS

  Capterra and G2 both REFUSE to list software whose only
  call to action is "contact us". They require a publicly
  available demo or trial. This page unblocks both listings.

  It also works at 2am when you are asleep and a plant
  manager is comparing options.


CHECK IT WORKED

  Open https://hsbfix.org/demo.html and click the
