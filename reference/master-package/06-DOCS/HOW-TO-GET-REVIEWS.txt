================================================================
 HOW TO GET REVIEWS - the templates and the timing
 PlantMaster Pro / HSB Fix Services
================================================================

You told me you have no paying customers yet. So this file is
for the moment you do. Keep it and come back to it.

Nothing here invents a review. Every word is designed to get a
REAL customer to write a real one, which is the only kind that
survives.


----------------------------------------------------------------
 WHEN TO ASK - this matters more than the wording
----------------------------------------------------------------

Ask right after a win. Not on a random Tuesday.

The four good moments:

  1. The AI solved a breakdown and saved them a callout.
     Ask the same day, while they are still impressed.

  2. Condition monitoring caught a bearing before it failed.
     This is your strongest story. Nobody else in the market
     does it from a phone microphone.

  3. End of the first month, if usage is healthy - meaning
     they are logging in most days and closing work orders.

  4. They say something nice on WhatsApp. This is the easiest
     one. When a customer writes "bohot acha kaam kar raha
     hai", reply and ask right then.

The four times NOT to ask:

  - Right after a bug or an outage.
  - When an invoice is overdue.
  - Before they have closed at least twenty work orders.
     They have nothing to say yet.
  - In the same message as an upsell.


----------------------------------------------------------------
 WHO TO ASK
----------------------------------------------------------------

Ask the maintenance manager, not the owner. The manager uses
it daily and can write specifics. Specific reviews rank higher
and convince more.

Ask one person per company. Multiple reviews from the same
company get filtered.

----------------------------------------------------------------
 IF THEY SAY YES BUT DO NOT KNOW WHAT TO WRITE
----------------------------------------------------------------

Most people stall here. Do not write it for them - that is
against the rules and it reads fake. Instead send prompts:

    No need to write an essay. Just answer whichever of
    these you feel like:

      - What were you using before, and what was annoying
        about it?
      - What does your team use it for most?
      - Has it caught anything you would otherwise have
        missed?
      - What should we improve?

That produces a specific, credible review without you putting
words in their mouth.


----------------------------------------------------------------
 HANDLING A BAD REVIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------

You will get one eventually. It is not a disaster - a profile
of nothing but five stars looks purchased, and buyers know it.

  Reply within 48 hours. On Capterra, replying is free.
  Thank them, do not argue, state what you changed.
  Never mention their company name or personal details.
  Fix the thing, then message them privately and tell them.
  Never ask them to take the review down.

A calm, specific reply to a three star review sells better
than the review itself costs you.

 THE TARGET
----------------------------------------------------------------

  First 3 reviews      the profile stops looking abandoned

At one review per happy customer, twenty customers gets you
there. That is the whole game.
