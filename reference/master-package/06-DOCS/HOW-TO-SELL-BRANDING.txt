  THE SITUATION TODAY
  -------------------

  The branding page already works. Logo, application name,
  colours, theme and pattern all save and apply correctly.

  But there is NO plan check on it anywhere. I searched both
  apps. Every company on every plan can brand itself right
  now, including the free 7-day trial.

  So nothing needs building. It needs a lock.


  WHAT YOU ARE SELLING — BE PRECISE
  ---------------------------------

  WORKS TODAY, per company:
    - Logo in the app header
    - Application name in the header and browser tab
    - Primary and secondary colours
    - Theme and background pattern
    - Branding carried onto documents and reports

  DOES NOT WORK, shared by everyone:
    - The icon and name on the phone home screen when the
      app is installed. One fixed file, manifest.webmanifest,
      says "PlantMaster Pro Multi-User" for all customers.
    - The login screen before sign-in. "PlantMaster Pro" is
      written into index.html.

  Sell it as "your branding inside the app and on your
  reports". Do NOT sell it as "your own app". A customer who
  pays for white-label, installs it, and sees your name on
  their home screen will feel misled. Full home-screen
  branding is a separate, larger job — see the last section.


  THE SPLIT — WHAT IS PAID AND WHAT STAYS FREE
  --------------------------------------------

  PAID (the "white_label" feature):
    Company logo
    Application name
    Primary colour
    Secondary colour
    Theme
    Pattern

  FREE ON EVERY PLAN:
    Plant display name
    Company address
    Contact number
    Company email
    Daily report time
    Document footer

  Reason: the free ones are operational, not branding. Every
  customer needs their own address and phone number on a work
  order, even on the cheapest plan. Locking those makes a
  cheap plan feel broken rather than making the paid plan
  feel valuable.


===============================================================
  STEP 1 — ADD THE FEATURE KEY (admin panel)
===============================================================

  File: app.js in the admin panel, around line 595.
  Add one line to the end of the MODULES list:

      { key:'white_label',  label:'Company branding (logo, name, colours)' }

  That is the whole change on the admin side.

  Because both plan editors are generated from MODULES, the
  tick-box appears automatically in:
    - Packages > edit a package        (applies to ALL customers)
    - Companies > edit one company     (applies to THAT company)

  This is exactly how your other 20 features already behave.


===============================================================
  STEP 2 — HOW TO SELL IT, BOTH WAYS
===============================================================

  BUNDLED INTO A PACKAGE
    Packages > Professional > tick "Company branding" > Save.
    Every customer on Professional gets it immediately.
    Leave it unticked on Basic, Essential and Trial.

  AS A PAID ADD-ON FOR ONE CUSTOMER
    Companies > the company > edit > tick "Company branding".

    IMPORTANT LIMITATION, know this before you promise it:
    the per-company editor can currently only take a feature
    AWAY, not add one. It works by writing a "block" row. So
    ticking a box the customer's package does not include
    will not switch it on.

    Two ways to handle an add-on customer today:

    (a) Move them up to a package that includes branding.
        Simplest, no code.

    (b) Make a copy of their package with branding ticked —
        for example "Essential + Branding" — and move that
        one customer onto it. This is the normal way SaaS
        businesses handle a bespoke deal and needs no code.

    If you would rather tick a box on any company regardless
    of package, that needs a grants table to mirror the
    existing blocks table. Ask and it can be added.


===============================================================
  STEP 3 — LOCK IT IN THE CUSTOMER APP
===============================================================

  This is the part that actually stops a customer using it.
  File: app.js in the customer app.

  3a. Read the entitlement when the profile page loads.

      In renderCompanyProfile(), before building the HTML,
      fetch the plan and check the flag:

          const ent = await sb.rpc('organization_plan_summary',
                          { p_organization_id: org.id });
          const canBrand = !!(ent.data?.plan?.features?.white_label);

  3b. For the six paid fields, when canBrand is false:
        - add the  disabled  attribute to the input
        - show the upgrade note described below

      Keep the fields VISIBLE and greyed out, not hidden.
      A locked field the customer can see is an advert for
      the upgrade. A hidden one teaches them nothing.

      Suggested wording for the note:

          Company branding is part of the Professional plan.
          Contact HSB Fix Services on WhatsApp to enable your
          own logo, name and colours.

      Link it to  https://wa.me/923162364074  so an interested
      customer reaches you in one tap.

  3c. Enforce it on save as well, not only in the form.

      In saveCompanyProfile(), if canBrand is false, strip the
      six paid keys out of the payload before the upsert:

          if(!canBrand){
            delete payload.app_name;
            delete payload.primary_color;
            delete payload.secondary_color;
            delete payload.theme;
            delete payload.pattern;
            // and skip the logo upload entirely
          }

      Without this, a customer could re-enable a disabled
      field with the browser's developer tools and save.


===============================================================
  HOW STRONG IS THIS LOCK
===============================================================

  Honest answer: it stops ordinary users, not a determined
  technical one.

  The customer app talks to the database directly, so someone
  who knows how could write to organization_settings from the
  browser console and bypass the app entirely.

  Proper enforcement is a database rule that refuses the write
  unless the company's plan includes white_label. That is the
  correct long-term fix and is worth adding once this is
  earning money.

  For now: your customers are plant and maintenance managers,
  not people hunting for a bypass. The UI lock is a reasonable
  place to launch from. Just know the difference between a
  lock and a deterrent.


===============================================================
  WHAT TO CHARGE
===============================================================

  Your current plans:
    Trial 7 days   free
    Basic          PKR 29 / month
    Essential      PKR 12,000 / month
    Professional   PKR 24,000 / month
    Enterprise     free (internal)

  Branding is a classic reason to move up a tier rather than
  a large standalone charge. Two sensible options:

    - Bundle it into Professional. It becomes a concrete
      reason to pay 24,000 instead of 12,000, and the jump
      pays for itself many times over versus a small add-on
      fee.

    - Or offer it to an Essential customer as a bespoke
      package at, say, PKR 3,000-5,000 a month, using method
      (b) in Step 2.

  Branding costs you nothing per customer to deliver — no
  storage to speak of, no AI calls. Price it on what it is
  worth to them, which is looking like their own system in
  front of their own management, not on what it costs you.

  Also worth knowing: a customer with their own logo and
  colours on the screen every day is markedly less likely to
  leave. It is a retention feature as much as a revenue one.


===============================================================
  LATER — FULL HOME-SCREEN WHITE-LABEL
===============================================================

  To make the installed app show the customer's own icon and
  name on their phone, you need:

    1. A manifest generated per company rather than the one
       fixed manifest.webmanifest file. It has to be served
       with the customer's name and icon in it, which means
       something dynamic — a small edge function, or a
       separate small file per branded customer.

    2. Icons in the required sizes, generated from whatever
       logo the customer uploads, at 192px and 512px plus a
       maskable version.

    3. The login screen reading the company's branding before
       sign-in. Harder than it sounds: the app does not know
       which company the visitor belongs to until they log
       in. Usually solved by giving each branded customer
       their own web address, for example
       customername.hsbfix.org.

  That third point is the real work, and it is what a
  customer paying for genuine white-label will eventually
  expect. Worth doing once two or three customers are paying
  for branding — not before.


===============================================================
  SUMMARY
===============================================================

  1. Add one line to MODULES in the admin app.js.
  2. Tick the box on Professional and Enterprise.
  3. Add the entitlement check and the save-side strip in the
     customer app.
  4. Sell it as in-app and document branding, not as a
     separate app.
  5. Revisit the home-screen manifest when customers are
     paying for it.
