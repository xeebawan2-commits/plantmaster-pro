DATABASE FUNCTIONS (RPCs) — PlantMaster Pro
Project: dpmmenwziplixrgylapy
Checked live: 15 September 2026

All of these are CONFIRMED PRESENT and in use by the apps.

A note on testing them: Postgres matches a function by its
ARGUMENT NAMES, not just its name. Calling one with an empty
request gives a misleading "404 not found" even when it exists.
Always send the real arguments.

    open_tickets, critical_tickets, open_incidents,

    complimentary_ends_at, created_at
